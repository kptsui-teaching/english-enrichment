包含月份的常用例句 Example Sentences

  1. 會議將於 12 月 15 日舉行。 → The conference will take place on December 15.
  2. 我在八月出生。 → I was born in August.
  3. 我的生日是 8 月 25 號。 → My birthday is on August 25th.
  4. 下一個公眾假期是什麼時候？7 月 4 日。 → When is the next public holiday? It's on July 4th.
  5. 下一次團隊會議是每月第一個星期一。 → When is the next team meeting? It's on the first Monday of every month.
